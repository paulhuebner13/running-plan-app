V61: Default week order is Tuesday Interval, Thursday Threshold, Saturday Easy, Sunday Long Run. Only after the immediately preceding calendar week has ended, if at least one run was completed, a missed Threshold may move before completed Intervals, and a missed Long Run may move from Sunday to Saturday. Easy is never specifically prioritized. All four runs, distances, ratings, and Gym data stay unchanged. Future weeks keep the default schedule until the relevant previous week closes.
